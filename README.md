# Festival of Ideas Putrajaya 2025
`Date: 4 - 9 Nov 2025`

`Venue: Putrajaya International Convention Centre (PICC)`
</div>

## Background
The relevance of this festival has remained true in terms of giving a platform for the discussion of the concept of creativity and knowledge acquisition for the whole population of mankind. The relevance of this festival also entailed giving a platform for discussion on issues under the concept of problem-solving not only in Malaysia but for the whole masses of the world, represented by the government, professionals, and citizens. It is a requirement within the context of the vision of this festival to create a society where knowledge appreciation and big ideas are revered. This festival remains relevant in marking the success not only of Malaysia but of the ‘City of Ideas’ itself, Putrajaya.
## Reflection
> _Me and some of my classmates attended the Putrajaya Festival of Ideas on 5th November 2025. There were many other university students who attended this event as well, like those from UM, UKM, UPM, UiTM, UTeM, and so on. To be honest, I thought I was just going there to be a passive listener, but this festival has proven me wrong in so many ways._
>
> _There are lots of things that we learn inside the classroom, and we do not have any background information regarding the application of these things. Being here in the festival, listening to the people who actually get to apply the things related to AI and green technology, I have a good grasp of the application of the things that I am learning in class and the skills that I need to gain to have a good job in the future. It made my anxiety a list of things to achieve. Of course, it is really inspiring, to a certain degree. Being among smart and passionate people, it’s making me excited about my own abilities. I have a good realization that I am learning inside the classroom not only for passing exams, which I have no idea that I am capable of actually doing cool things in the future because of learning such things, as I would be one of the people in the Malaysia of the future. There, I have a good concept of things that I need to study, and it’s really good._
>
> _Special thanks to the Minister of Higher Education for initiating this amazing experience._
