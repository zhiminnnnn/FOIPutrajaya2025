# Festival of Ideas Putrajaya 2025
`Date: 4 - 9 Nov 2025`

`Venue: Putrajaya International Convention Centre (PICC)`
</div>

## 💡Background
The relevance of this festival has remained true in terms of giving a platform for the discussion of the concept of creativity and knowledge acquisition for the whole population of mankind. The relevance of this festival also entailed giving a platform for discussion on issues under the concept of problem-solving not only in Malaysia but for the whole masses of the world, represented by the government, professionals, and citizens. It is a requirement within the context of the vision of this festival to create a society where knowledge appreciation and big ideas are revered. This festival remains relevant in marking the success not only of Malaysia but of the ‘City of Ideas’ itself, Putrajaya.
## ✍🏻Reflection
>The Putrajaya Festival of Ideas was attended on 5th November 2025. A wide representation of the student body was seen, as participants from UM, UKM, UPM, UiTM, UTeM, and other universities were present. Although the event was initially expected to be a passive listening experience, those assumptions were proven wrong in many ways.
>
>While many subjects are learned within the classroom, background information regarding their practical application is often not provided. At this festival, a deeper understanding was gained by listening to experts who actually apply concepts related to AI and green technology. The real-world applications of classroom lessons were clarified, and the specific skills required for future employment were identified.
>
>As a result of this experience, personal anxiety was transformed into a structured list of goals to be achieved. Inspiration was felt to a great degree by being surrounded by smart and passionate individuals, and excitement was generated regarding personal potential. It was realized that learning is not conducted merely for the sake of passing exams; rather, it was understood that "cool things" can be accomplished in the future. A vision was formed of being among those who will shape the Malaysia of the future.
>
>A clear concept of the necessary areas of study has now been established. Finally, special thanks are extended to the Minister of Higher Education for the initiation of this amazing experience.
